# Roadmap

OpenPaperwork grows one standard at a time. This is what we would like to add next. It has no
dates and makes no promises. The order follows demand and contributions, so a pull request or a
clear issue moves an item up.

## Next, by demand

- **Forms you fill in.** The form somebody else issues, filled in and checked against the rules the form itself names.
- **Paperwork that comes in.** The files that arrive in a folder, read and checked, answered with the document they ask for.
- **Compliance records.** The records a rule asks a company to keep, written and checked the same way as the rest of the library.
- **Company details checked against the public registers.** The seller's and buyer's numbers and names, checked against VIES and the national company registers before the file is made.

## Standards

- **More e-invoice countries.** National invoice rules and formats beyond EN 16931, one country
  at a time.
- **More citation styles and paper formats.** Further reference styles, thesis formats from
  universities, and journal and conference templates.
- **More trade documents.** Further documents a business sends and receives, each tied to its
  published rules.

[Add a standard](ADD_A_STANDARD.md) lists the ones we would like and walks through adding one.

## Agents

- **A hook that forces the check** in the apps that have none we can show working. Today the agent runs the check because the skill asks it to.
- **More agents**, each with one real run written up.
