# The OpenPaperwork and Nodejam names and marks

The code and the library are open source under the [Apache 2.0 licence](LICENSE). The names
OpenPaperwork and Nodejam and the op mark are not: they belong to Nodejam AB, and the licence does not
grant them (its section 6). This page says what you may do with them without asking.

## Fine without asking

- Say what is true: that your product works with OpenPaperwork, uses its packs, or was built on it.
- Pass on an unchanged copy of this repository under its own name, licence and notices.
- Write about OpenPaperwork, review it, teach it, or show it in a talk, with its name and mark.
- Link to this repository with its name.

## Ask us first

- Naming a product, service, company, plugin, package or domain with OpenPaperwork or Nodejam, or with
  a name that could be mistaken for them.
- Selling or hosting OpenPaperwork, changed or not, under its name or mark. Under your own name, the
  licence allows it.
- Using the op mark as your logo or app icon, or changing it.
- Anything that suggests Nodejam made, approved or supports your work when it did not.

## A fork

A fork is welcome under the licence. If it changes what the packs make or check, give it a name of its
own and say plainly that it is based on OpenPaperwork, so nobody takes a change of yours for ours.

Ask at hello@nodejam.com. This page is not legal advice, and it does not limit what the law allows anyway,
such as a fair, accurate mention of the name.
