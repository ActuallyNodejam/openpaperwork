Spanish, Italian and Polish laws, official texts and e-invoice formats, used by the e-invoice pack

Official texts are not protected by copyright in these countries:
- Spain: texto refundido de la Ley de Propiedad Intelectual (Real Decreto Legislativo 1/1996) art. 13, legal
  and regulatory provisions are not objects of intellectual property.
- Italy: legge 22 aprile 1941, n. 633, art. 5, the act does not apply to the texts of official acts of the
  state and of public administrations.
- Poland: ustawa z dnia 4 lutego 1994 r. o prawie autorskim i prawach pokrewnych art. 4 pkt 1 and 2,
  normative acts and official documents, materials, signs and symbols are not objects of copyright.

Sources, read 2026-10-09:
- Spain: Reglamento por el que se regulan las obligaciones de facturación (Real Decreto 1619/2012), art. 6.1
  (m: «inversión del sujeto pasivo» quoted), art. 6.5 and art. 15, BOE consolidated text, last change in
  force 7 December 2023; Real Decreto 1007/2023, art. 1, 3 and 7 and disposición final cuarta as amended by
  Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 of 2 December 2025 (1 January 2027 and 1 July 2027); Real Decreto 1065/2007
  art. 19 and 20 and Orden EHA/451/2008 art. 2 to 5 on the NIF; Ley 25/2013 art. 4, 5 and disposición
  adicional segunda (boe.es).
- Italy: DPR 26 ottobre 1972, n. 633, art. 17, 17-ter, 21 and 26, the text in force on 9 October 2026
  («inversione contabile» quoted); D.Lgs. 19 gennaio 2026, n. 10 (Testo unico IVA), which repeals DPR
  633/1972 from 1 January 2027, art. 64, 65, 72, 77 and 92; DM 3 aprile 2013, n. 55 (normattiva.it); DM
  17 giugno 2014 art. 6 (gazzettaufficiale.it).
- Poland: ustawa z dnia 11 marca 2004 r. o podatku od towarów i usług, art. 106e, 106ga, 106gb, 106j, 145l
  and 145m («odwrotne obciążenie», «mechanizm podzielonej płatności» and «metoda kasowa» quoted), the
  consolidated text kept by the Chancellery of the Sejm, as of 20 July 2026 (isap.sejm.gov.pl).

Formats (nothing of these is in this repository; each schema is fetched at a pinned SHA-256 and run):
- Facturae 3.2.2, facturae.gob.es. Reuse of the site's contents is allowed under Ley 37/2007 with the source
  named. Origen de los datos: Ministerio de Hacienda y Administraciones Públicas - Ministerio de Industria,
  Energía y Turismo.
- FatturaPA, schema 1.2.3 and technical specifications 1.4, valid from 1 April 2025, fatturapa.gov.it. The
  site reserves every right in its contents; the format itself is allegato A of DM 55/2013.
- The logical structure FA(3) of the Krajowy System e-Faktur, Ministry of Finance, published as an
  electronic document template at crd.gov.pl/wzor/2025/06/25/13775/ and in github.com/CIRFMF/ksef-docs. The
  install writes two copies of it for a validator without a network: one whose schemaLocation attributes
  name the file beside them, and one where every maxOccurs above 100 is unbounded as well.

Not read from an official text: the check characters of the Spanish NIF, the Italian partita IVA and the
Polish NIP (the pack refuses on the form of the number and only warns on the check character); the words
«scissione dei pagamenti» of DM 23 gennaio 2015 (read in the FatturaPA specification); the stamp-duty
threshold of DPR 642/1972 (the pack never decides whether stamp duty is due).

Changes: the requirements are restated in our own words as builder refusals and page checks in
