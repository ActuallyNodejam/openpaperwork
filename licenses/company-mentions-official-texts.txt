Official statutory texts behind the company details on letters and trade documents for Belgium, the
Netherlands, Austria and Ireland (wave 8) and for Spain, Italy and Poland (wave 9): skills/_shared/letter.py
company_missing and company_lines, skills/_checks/letter_check.py LETTER-COMPANY, skills/trade-documents
(company_warning). Each is cited by
article and restated in our own words; no text is copied whole. Only the official publications are
authentic.

Belgium: Code des sociétés et des associations / Wetboek van vennootschappen en verenigingen (loi du
23 mars 2019), art. 2:20: the name, the legal form, the seat, the enterprise number, "RPM" or "RPR"
with the court of the seat, the e-mail address and website where there are any, and liquidation.
Justel, https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be, read 2026-10-09. Official acts are not protected by
copyright in Belgium (Code de droit économique art. XI.172 § 2).

Netherlands: Handelsregisterwet 2007 art. 27 (the KvK number on letters, orders, invoices, quotations
and other announcements) and Burgerlijk Wetboek Boek 2 art. 186 (a BV's full name and seat), versions
in force on 2026-10-09, https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0021777 and BWBR0003045, read that day. Laws
are not protected by copyright in the Netherlands (Auteurswet art. 11).

Austria: Unternehmensgesetzbuch § 14 (1) to (4), as amended by BGBl. I Nr. 133/2024: the firm, the
legal form, the seat, the Firmenbuch number and court, on business letters, order forms and websites.
The GmbH-Gesetz asks for no managing directors there. RIS, https://www.ris.bka.gv.at, read 2026-10-09.
Laws are not protected by copyright in Austria (Urheberrechtsgesetz § 7).

Ireland: Companies Act 2014 (No. 38 of 2014) s.151 (1) and (2): every director's present forename or
initials and surname, any former names, and nationality if not Irish; the name and legal form, the
place of registration and number, and the registered office. Subsection (1) binds business letters,
subsection (2) business letters and order forms. The European Communities (Companies) Regulations 1973
(S.I. No. 163 of 1973), whose regulation 9 first carried the rule, were revoked whole by Schedule 2
Part 2 of the 2014 Act. https://www.irishstatutebook.ie, read 2026-10-09. Contains Oireachtas
information licensed under the Oireachtas (Open Data) PSI Licence, which incorporates the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.

Spain (wave 9): Código de Comercio (Real Decreto de 22 de agosto de 1885), art. 24: a business subject to
registration states, on all its documents, correspondence, order notes and invoices, its domicile and
the particulars of its entry in the Registro Mercantil; a company also its legal form and, where it
applies, that it is in liquidation; a mention of the capital names the subscribed and the paid-up
capital. BOE consolidated text (BOE-A-1885-6627), https://www.boe.es, read 2026-10-09; informative only,
with no legal value. Not the object of intellectual property (Ley de Propiedad Intelectual art. 13).
Basado en datos de la Agencia Estatal Boletín Oficial del Estado.

Italy (wave 9): Codice civile (regio decreto 16 marzo 1942, n. 262), art. 2250, in force since 29 July
2009: the acts and correspondence of a company that must be entered in the registro delle imprese state
its seat, the register office and the number of its entry; an S.p.A., S.a.p.a. or S.r.l. its capital as
paid up and as the last balance sheet shows it; that it is in liquidation; and an S.p.A. or S.r.l. that
it has a sole shareholder. Normattiva, https://www.normattiva.it, read 2026-10-09. Outside the copyright
law (legge 22 aprile 1941, n. 633, art. 5).

Poland (wave 9): Ustawa z dnia 15 września 2000 r. – Kodeks spółek handlowych, art. 206 § 1 (a spółka z
ograniczoną odpowiedzialnością) and art. 374 § 1 (a spółka akcyjna): letters and commercial orders, on
paper or electronic, and the company's website state its name, seat and address, the registry court and
the number of its entry, its NIP, its share capital (an S.A. also the paid-up capital) and the group of
companies it belongs to. The Sejm's consolidated text of 3 June 2026 (t.j. Dz. U. z 2024 r. poz. 18,
amended to Dz. U. z 2026 r. poz. 644), https://isap.sejm.gov.pl, read 2026-10-09. Not the object of
copyright (ustawa o prawie autorskim i prawach pokrewnych art. 4 pkt 1).
