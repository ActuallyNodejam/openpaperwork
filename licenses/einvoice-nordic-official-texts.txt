Norwegian, Danish and Finnish laws and regulations on invoices, used by the e-invoice pack

Laws, regulations and official decisions are not protected by copyright in these countries:
- Norway: åndsverkloven (LOV-2018-06-15-40) § 14, "lover, forskrifter, ... vedtak og andre beslutninger
  av offentlig myndighet" are free of copyright.
- Denmark: ophavsretsloven § 9, love, administrative forskrifter and
  similar official documents are not subject to copyright.
- Finland: tekijänoikeuslaki (404/1961) 9 §, lait ja asetukset and official decisions are free of
  copyright.

Sources, read 2026-10-09:
- Forskrift om bokføring (bokføringsforskriften), FOR-2004-12-01-1558, kapittel 5-1, §§ 5-1-1 to 5-1-5
  (Lovdata). The reverse-charge mark "Omvendt avgiftsplikt – Merverdiavgift ikke beregnet" (§ 5-1-1
  nr. 7) and the word "Foretaksregisteret" (§ 5-1-2, which still points at § 10-2 of the 1985 register act, repealed on
  1 January 2026; the duty now stands in § 7-2 of lov 20. juni 2025 nr. 106 om Foretaksregisteret) are quoted.
- Bekendtgørelse om merværdiafgift (momsbekendtgørelsen) § 58, stk. 1 and 2, as Skattestyrelsens
  Juridiske Vejledning A.B.3.3.1.4 restates it; Erhvervsstyrelsen, "Dokumentstrategi" for NemHandel
  (5 March 2026) and its høringsnotat (nemhandel.dk), for OIOUBL 3.0 being dropped in favour of
  a Peppol-based Nemhandel BIS 4 from 2028-2029.
- Arvonlisäverolaki (1501/1993) 209 e § and 209 f §, as Verohallinto's guidance "Laskutusvaatimukset
  arvonlisäverotuksessa" restates them. The mark "Käännetty verovelvollisuus" is quoted.
