Irish VAT law and Revenue guidance on invoices, used by the e-invoice pack

Contains Irish Public Sector Information licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0) licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010 ss. 66 and 67 and the Value-Added Tax Regulations 2010
  (S.I. No. 639 of 2010) reg. 20, Irish Statute Book (Office of the Attorney General). Re-use of the Irish
  Statute Book is under the Oireachtas (Open Data) PSI Licence, which incorporates the Creative Commons
  Attribution 4.0 International License (irishstatutebook.ie, Open Data Initiative).
- Revenue has no single invoice manual; its invoice guidance is two pages on revenue.ie: "What information
  is required on a VAT invoice?" (published 16 March 2026) and "What information is required on a VAT
  credit note?" (published 6 October 2026). Information provided courtesy of the Revenue Commissioners
  under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence (Revenue, "Re-use of public
  sector information").
- Revenue, "VAT Modernisation: Implementation of eInvoicing in Ireland" (the roadmap of the phases:
  November 2028, November 2029, July 2030), under the same licence.
- Companies Act 2014 s. 151, Irish Statute Book.
