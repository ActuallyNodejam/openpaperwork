Working title: Sleep and examination performance in university students

## Points I want the Introduction and Related Work to cover

1. A lot of university students sleep too little, especially around exams.
   I need a study with real numbers for this.
2. Students who sleep longer or better tend to get better grades. I know
   most of this is correlational; say so.
3. It may not only be how long students sleep but how regular their sleep
   times are. I think there is a study on sleep regularity and grades.
4. Caffeine and screens late in the evening push sleep later. One source
   for each would be good.
5. Do sleep programmes for students actually help? I have heard the
   evidence is mixed. Report what you find, even if it is disappointing.

## What our own study will do (one sentence at the end of Related Work)

We will measure sleep with wrist trackers and compare it with real exam
grades from the same week. We have no results yet, so don't write any.
