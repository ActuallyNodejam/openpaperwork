Hand hygiene before and after patient contact on a surgical ward

Hand hygiene is the single habit most often linked with fewer healthcare-associated infections (Danforth 2021). Even so, staff on busy wards miss about one in three opportunities (Kowalczyk-Ames 2020).
Placing alcohol rub at the point of care makes it easier to clean hands at the right moment (Example Infection Network 2025). Feedback on observed compliance also helps when it is given to the whole team (Danforth 2021).
Reflection models can help a student understand why they skipped a step under pressure (Halvorsen 2019). On my placement I saw that the rub dispensers by the bays were often empty at night, which matches what Okeke-Brandt found on other wards (Okeke-Brandt 2022).
I will check the dispensers at the start of every shift and raise the night-time gap at the ward meeting.
