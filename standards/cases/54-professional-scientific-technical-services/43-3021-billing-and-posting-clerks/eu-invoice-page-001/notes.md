# eu-invoice-page-001 - the readable half

Domestic German supply at the 19 percent standard rate (sections 1(1) no. 1 and 12(1) UStG, read today at gesetze-im-internet.de). The page is checked against the order data: every number must trace to it, no field may appear that it does not carry, and the invoice number, dates, parties, VAT identifiers, payment account, lines, totals and VAT breakdown must all be present.
