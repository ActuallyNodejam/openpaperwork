# eu-invoice-plain-001 - tax treatment

Domestic supply of goods inside Germany, seller and buyer both established in DE.

- Taxable supply: section 1(1) no. 1 UStG (German VAT Act).
- Rate: the standard rate of 19 percent, section 12(1) UStG.
- Invoice content: section 14(4) UStG, carried by the EN 16931 semantic model.
- EN 16931 shape: document type code 380 (commercial invoice), VAT category S,
  one VAT breakdown entry (BG-23) at 19 percent on the full taxable amount.

Cross-border business-to-business supply is the reverse-charge case, not this one.
