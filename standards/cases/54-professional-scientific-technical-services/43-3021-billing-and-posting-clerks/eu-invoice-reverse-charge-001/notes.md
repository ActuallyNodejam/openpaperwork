# eu-invoice-reverse-charge-001 - tax treatment

Cross-border business-to-business supply of services: the supplier is established in
Germany, the customer in France, and both are taxable persons acting for their business.

- The supplier does not charge VAT on the invoice.
- The customer accounts for the VAT on the supply, under the reverse-charge mechanism.
- No article number is cited here: the official text of Directive 2006/112/EC was not
  read today, so per the citation rule the article number stays out until it is read.
- EN 16931 shape: document type code 380, VAT category AE at 0 percent, one breakdown
  entry (BG-23) at 0 percent on the full taxable amount, and the treatment stated in an
  invoice note (BT-22) that travels with the XML.
