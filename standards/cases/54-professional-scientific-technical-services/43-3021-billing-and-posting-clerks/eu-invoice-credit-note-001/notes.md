# eu-invoice-credit-note-001 - tax treatment

Credit note for a returned item on invoice INV-2026-0007, domestic Germany.

- The credit reduces the taxable amount of the original supply by one chair at 19 percent.
- Rule: the correction follows the same taxable supply as the original invoice (section
  1(1) no. 1 UStG) at the standard rate of 19 percent (section 12(1) UStG), and the
  document carries the original invoice number so the customer can match it.
- EN 16931 shape: document type code 381 (credit note), VAT category S at 19 percent,
  one breakdown entry (BG-23), the original invoice referenced (BT-25) inside the header
  settlement, and the reason in an invoice note (BT-22).
