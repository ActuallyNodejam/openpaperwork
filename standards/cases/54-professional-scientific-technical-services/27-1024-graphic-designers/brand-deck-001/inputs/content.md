Fictional studio pitch for a harbour museum. Six slides, one per section.

1. Opening. Northwind Studio proposes a new visitor route for the Harbour Museum, opening in May 2027, built around three working boats and the people who sailed them.

2. The problem. Visitor numbers fell from 82,000 in 2023 to 64,000 in 2025. Exit interviews with 310 visitors say the route is confusing and that half of them missed the boat hall entirely.

3. The idea. One marked route in a single direction, starting at the boat hall. Each boat gets one story, one object and one voice recording, so no stop takes longer than four minutes.

4. Budget. The total is EUR 180,000: route and signage 60,000, three story stations 75,000, audio production 25,000, and a 20,000 reserve. No building work is included.

5. Timeline. Concept approval in November 2026, production from December to March, installation in April and opening on 2 May 2027.

6. The ask. Approve the concept on 14 November 2026 and name one museum contact for weekly reviews.
