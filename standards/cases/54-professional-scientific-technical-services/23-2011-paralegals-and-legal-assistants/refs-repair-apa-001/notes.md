# refs-repair-apa-001 - what is wrong in the draft

The draft has three faults a reader would notice: numbered citations instead of APA 7
author-date citations, a reference list in citation order rather than alphabetical, and
an entry with no DOI and a different date position. The repaired answer is shipped beside
it as report.docx so the case is checkable end to end. APA Style is a work of the American
Psychological Association; the guideline paraphrases the rules and copies no text from the
Publication Manual.
