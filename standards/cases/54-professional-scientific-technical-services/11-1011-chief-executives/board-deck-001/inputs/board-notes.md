# Notes for the board update, third quarter 2026 (Example Software Ltd)

Meeting: 22 October 2026, board of directors. Confidential.

## Results
- Annual recurring revenue by quarter, EUR million: Q3 2025 14.1, Q4 2025 15.0, Q1 2026 16.2, Q2 2026 17.3, Q3 2026 18.4.
- Plan for Q3 2026 was 17.4. Net new recurring revenue 1.1 against 0.9 planned.
- Gross margin 78.0% of revenue (plan 77.0). Hosting costs flat.
- Net revenue retention 104.0% over twelve months (plan 103.0).
- Cash at quarter end EUR 9.4 million (plan 9.0).
- Growth came from larger accounts adding seats. List prices unchanged since 2024.

## Customers
- Accounts lost in the quarter, % of accounts at its start, Q3 2025 to Q3 2026:
  small accounts 2.1, 2.6, 3.1, 3.7, 4.2; mid-sized 1.4, 1.3, 1.4, 1.3, 1.4; large 0.5, 0.4, 0.5, 0.4, 0.4.
- Plan for small-account churn was 2.5% a quarter.
- 62 small accounts cancelled in Q3. 24 had not finished onboarding. 14 named price as the reason.

## Plan
- Two options: guided onboarding (a specialist sets up each new small account; EUR 0.4 million in year one;
  pays back within 7 months; can be undone after the pilot; ready December 2026) or a 15% price cut for small
  accounts (EUR 0.9 million of revenue; price is only the second reason; not paid back within the year; January 2027).
- Guided onboarding in four steps: welcome call on day one, guided setup in week one, first report together by
  week three, health check on day 60.
- Dates: two specialists hired in November 2026; pilot with 40 new accounts from December 2026; the board reviews
  pilot churn in February 2027 (goes on only if under 3%); roll-out to all new small accounts in March 2027.
- The pilot is judged on: churn under 3% a quarter, nine in ten accounts through setup within a week (six in ten
  today), cost within EUR 0.4 million.
- Next steps: board approves EUR 0.4 million (22 October 2026); head of customer success hires two specialists
  (30 November 2026) and starts the pilot (1 December 2026); the chief financial officer reports pilot churn
  (18 February 2027).

Source of the figures: management accounts, 30 September 2026; billing system and exit survey, same date.
