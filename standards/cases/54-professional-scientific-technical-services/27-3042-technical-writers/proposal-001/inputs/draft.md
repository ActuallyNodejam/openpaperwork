Fictional project draft. Project name: RIVERMESH. Call: fictional topic on climate adaptation.

Objectives. RIVERMESH will cut flood-warning time for small rivers from six hours to one. Objective one is a low-cost sensor mesh of 300 units on three pilot rivers. Objective two is an open forecasting model that runs on the mesh data. Objective three is a warning service used by at least 20 municipalities by month 30. Today's services cover large rivers; small catchments are warned late or not at all.

How we will do it. We start at technology readiness level 4 and end at level 7. Year one builds and calibrates the sensors in a laboratory flume and on one river. Year two deploys the mesh on three rivers and trains the forecasting model on two flood seasons. Year three runs the warning service with municipalities and measures warning time against the six-hour baseline. All software and the forecasting model are published as open source and the data follow FAIR principles.

Why it matters. Outcome: municipalities on small rivers get a one-hour warning service. Wider effect: fewer flood losses and a reusable open model for other regions. The main barrier is municipal procurement cycles; we address it with three signed pilot agreements before the project starts.

Spreading and using the results. We publish in open-access journals, release the model under an open licence, run two workshops a year for municipal emergency planners and brief national water agencies in the three partner countries. Fjord Logistics leads commercial use of the sensor unit.

Work plan. Work package 1, management, months 1 to 36, led by Northwind Studio. Work package 2, sensor mesh, months 1 to 18, led by Fjord Logistics. Work package 3, forecasting model, months 6 to 30, led by Delta Hydrology Institute. Work package 4, warning service and pilots, months 18 to 36, led by Northwind Studio. Total effort is 310 person-months. The main risk is sensor loss in floods; spare units are budgeted at 15 percent.

The consortium. Northwind Studio designs public digital services and coordinates. Delta Hydrology Institute has run river forecasting for 20 years. Fjord Logistics builds ruggedised field hardware. Together they cover hardware, modelling and service design; no partner duplicates another.
