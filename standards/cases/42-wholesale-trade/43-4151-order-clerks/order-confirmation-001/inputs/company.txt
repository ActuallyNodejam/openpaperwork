How we do paperwork
- Prices are in SEK excluding VAT unless a price list says otherwise.
- Swedish customers: VAT 25% on equipment and supplies, 12% on goods at the reduced rate.
- Customers outside the EU: no Swedish VAT (export), and they pay in advance.
- Each line is rounded to two decimals, then VAT is worked out once per VAT rate on the sum of that rate's lines.
- Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of the quotation.
- Payment for Swedish customers: 30 days net from invoice date.
- We deliver by road. Standard delivery term for Swedish customers: delivered to the customer's address, unloading by the customer
  (that is DAP under Incoterms 2020). If the customer collects from our warehouse in Gothenburg and we load their vehicle, it is FCA.
