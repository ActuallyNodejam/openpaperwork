Ward 7B falls audit, Apr-Sep before the move to single rooms vs Oct-Mar after. Sister Hana Okonkwo-Reyes.
Falls: 14 before, 23 after. 9 of the 23 between 22:00 and 06:00. Injuries: 2 before, 4 after (none severe).
Standard: every fall gets a post-fall review within 24 h. Met 12/14 before, 17/23 after.
Recommendations: night-time intentional rounding hourly; bed sensors in the four rooms furthest from the station; re-audit in six months.
