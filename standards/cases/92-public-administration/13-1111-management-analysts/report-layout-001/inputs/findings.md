Title: Permit processing review, autumn 2026

## Summary
The fictional municipality of Älvby asked for a review of how long building permits take. Median handling time was 41 days in 2025 and 33 days in the first eight months of 2026. The statutory target is 70 days, and 96 percent of 2026 cases met it. Three steps cause most of the waiting: completeness checks, neighbour consultation and archive retrieval.

## Method
We read 240 closed cases from January to August 2026, 30 per month, drawn at random from the case system. We timed each step from the system log and interviewed six handlers and two managers. We did not study appeals, and we did not measure applicant satisfaction.

## Findings
Completeness checks take a median of 9 days because 38 percent of applications arrive without a site plan. Neighbour consultation takes a median of 14 days and is fixed by the notice period. Archive retrieval takes a median of 5 days, since older drawings are stored off site. Decision writing takes a median of 3 days. The step medians are in the table.

## Recommendations
Publish a site plan checklist on the application page and reject incomplete applications within two working days. Scan the 500 most requested archive folders. Keep the consultation period as it is; it is set by law.

## Next steps
The building office decides on the checklist on 2026-11-10. The archive scanning needs a budget decision from the municipal board on 2026-12-01. We will measure handling time again in May 2027.
