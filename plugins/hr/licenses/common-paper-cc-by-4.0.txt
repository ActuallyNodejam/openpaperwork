Changes:
- All three are set in Word from the repositories' Markdown; Common Paper's links to the Cover Page
  are shown as shaded Variables; invisible direction marks are dropped.
- Mutual NDA, Section 9: "the laws of the State of" reads "the laws of", and "the federal or state
  courts located in" reads "the courts of", so the parties can choose a law and courts outside the
  United States. The Cover Page sets the Standard Terms out in full instead of incorporating the
  posted version, shows only the options chosen, and adds each party's registration number.
- DPA and Cloud Service Agreement: the repositories hold no Cover Page; the Cover Pages are our own
  arrangement of the Variables the Standard Terms name (for the DPA in the order of Annexes I to
  III of the EEA SCCs).
Every generated agreement prints Common Paper's notice, the licence link and these changes at its end.
