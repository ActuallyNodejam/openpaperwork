# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want finished work to come out right: contributors,
maintainers and anyone who opens an issue. Everyone here is owed the same plain respect,
whatever their background, experience or first language.

## What we expect

- Talk about the work: the file, the rule, the evidence. Not the person.
- Assume the other person means well, and ask before you conclude they do not.
- Say what you tried and what you did not test. Nobody is expected to know everything.
- Accept a "no" from a maintainer when it comes with a reason, and give a reason with yours.
- Use invented or redistributable data. Never post another person's private details.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or sexual language and attention of any kind.
- Remarks that put someone down for who they are.
- Publishing private information, or anything from a customer's or employer's files.
- Repeatedly pushing after a clear answer, or flooding issues with generated text.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere else
someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

Write to [hello@nodejam.com](mailto:hello@nodejam.com) with what happened and a link if
there is one. Reports are read by the maintainers at Nodejam and kept private. If your
report is about a maintainer, say so, and the other maintainer handles it.

## What happens next

A maintainer looks at the report and answers the person who sent it. Depending on what
happened, the result is a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a
permanent one. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break
this code.

A security problem is not a conduct report: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
